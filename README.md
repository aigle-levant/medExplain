# MedExplain (Mobile & Full-Stack Platform)

AI-powered healthcare platform that simplifies medical reports, explains medical terminology, manages medicine reminders, and provides hospital discovery.

## Features
- **MedExplain AI Medical Lab Report Analyzer**: Bilingual English & Tamil analysis for medical lab reports and blood tests (Groq, Gemini, OpenRouter, Ollama).
- **MedRemind Smart Medicine Reminders & Prescription OCR**: Scan prescriptions, parse medicine dosages, meal relations, and schedule medicine alerts & doctor appointments.
- **MedLexicon Medical Terminology & Medicines Directory**: Search medical terms, anatomical body systems, and comprehensive medicine dosing, uses, and side effects.
- **Healthcare & Hospital Discovery**: Find nearby hospitals with emergency availability, ratings, distance, and direct contacts.
- **React Native Android Mobile App**: Unified mobile application with native experience for all features.
